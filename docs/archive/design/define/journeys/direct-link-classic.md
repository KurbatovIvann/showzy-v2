# Direct Link — Classic UI Journey

> Linear: SHO-27 · Context: Public preview → Customer company  
> Also applies: `entry-path-conventions.md`

## Purpose and path

Open a company/product link through a limited public preview, preserve its
target through install/sign-in, and continue into independently authorized
company/cart state.

1. Open the Universal/App Link.
2. Route to the app or small install landing and retain the target.
3. A typed `public` resolver verifies current publication.
4. Show only the limited published company/product preview.
5. User chooses Continue, chat, or cart action.
6. Sign in where required with target/local cart intent preserved.
7. A `customer` action resolves the target again.
8. Open full current company/product state.
9. Add an available variant to the canonical company cart.
10. Create no CRM row; later checkout links/creates it.

## Classic ↔ AI

**Ask AI** transfers only stable target and resolved scope. AI can reopen the
same profile/product/cart. Authentication and dense variant choice remain
classic.

## Journey-specific recovery and evaluation

Not-found reveals no private existence. Unpublished company suppresses cached
details. Unavailable product may offer the current published company. Failed
install continuation offers deterministic **Open app** recovery.

Internally verify company/product links with and without install/sign-in, exact
target restoration, public-data limits, canonical cart, and no target-ID grant.
